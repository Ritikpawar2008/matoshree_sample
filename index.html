About Matoshree Lawns

Peshwai Elegance Meets
Contemporary Splendor

Nestled amid serene landscapes, Matoshree Lawns was conceived as a tribute to Maharashtra’s storied architectural and wedding legacy. From the resonant echoes of the traditional Tutari welcoming the Baarat to the sacred pheras around the Vedic Vedi Mandap, we orchestrate weddings that reflect timeless royalty, genuine warmth, and cultural reverence.

Every space is thoughtfully curated — combining expansive manicured greenery with regal pavilions, warm ambient chandeliers, and private air-conditioned suites — ensuring every celebration is unhurried and unforgettable.

  • ✦
    Traditional Tutari & Shehnai Welcome Courtyard
    An authentic ceremonial gateway designed for grand Baarat processions and welcome rituals.
  • ✦
    Sacred Vedic Vedi Mandap Architecture
    Purpose-built holy platforms honoring sacred traditional Maharashtrian marriage customs.
  • ✦
    Pure Maharashtrian & Global Royal Hospitality
    Dedicated banquet culinary complexes catering to authentic feasts, Ukadiche Modak, and modern gourmet spreads.
